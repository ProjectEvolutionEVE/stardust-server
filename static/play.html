Stardust - Rebuilding Home

FLIGHT PROTOTYPE · WORKING TITLE

Four rings of debris circle the Sun, where the inner planets used to be. The Sun is the only light. Distant debris stays dark until you're close enough to see it, and small fields only show up when you're nearly on top of them.

Fly: press and hold anywhere (finger, or the left mouse button). Holding flies you forward, and the screen works like a joystick from where you pressed: pull back to raise the nose, push up to dip it, right or left to bank. Let go and you drift to a stop.

Scanner: listen. A soft hiss means nothing ahead. A tone rises out of it as you point at a debris field, higher and clearer the closer you aim and the nearer you get. Let go to coast and look. Point dead at a return to take a bearing, move off to one side, and sweep it again: where the two cross, the field locks and the scanner goes quiet.

Lost? While you hold the stick, a faint gold glow on its rim shows which way the nearest debris field lies, even before you can see or hear it. Turn until the whole rim glows: the field is dead ahead.

Collect: the autopilot parks you in front of the field. Tap COLLECT and pieces drift aboard one at a time. Each piece has a real size, shape and weight. It only goes in if its shape fits your hold and there's room left for it. Tap HOLD (top left) to see what you carry.

Warp: the distances are real, so tap WARP (top right) to cross the solar system in seconds: to any of the four debris rings (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars), the Starship, the wormhole, Jupiter or Saturn. Inara tells you if you drift out of the ring you're working.

Home: warp to the Starship, by the wormhole beyond Jupiter, then double-tap it. The autopilot lines you up in front of the bow, then flies you in underneath it, past the lit command deck, into the bay. Press and hold to launch again.

Unload: once docked, tap UNLOAD. Your pieces move one at a time from your ship's hold into your own space aboard the Starship; watch both bars. Your space there is limited: when it's full, the rest stays in your hold. Launching stops the transfer, and what has already moved stays moved. Each unload gives you a receipt with a code. Tap SAVE RECEIPT to keep a copy on your device. While docked, tap QUARTERS to open your Private Quarters, where the receipts log lists every transfer.

Double-tap to fly to the locked field on autopilot, or double-tap any field or landmark you can see. Press and hold to take the controls back.

Keyboard too: WS forward / back · AD slide · RF up / down · QE roll · Shift boost · Space brake · H this help

BOOST (bottom right) speeds you up while you hold it.

The markers point to the Starship, the wormhole, the Sun and the gas giants when they're off screen.
