# Blueprint Ideas: a beginner's roadmap (approved by Eric 10-24)

**SECRET (Eric 10-24): an Easter egg.** Revealed as a surprise on passing the test on **The Blueprint Designer's Handbook** (CLAUDE.md rule 31); never mentioned or hinted at anywhere a player can see before then. It is a book of IDEAS, never
designs: every design is the player's own (rule 11). Each group runs from beginner ideas (anyone can picture them) to
advanced ones. The library books listed are the ones a designer would likely need (rule 23).

> Your Starship is full of things nobody has designed yet. Here are some places to start. None of them is the only way,
> and most of the best ideas aren't in this booklet at all.

## 1. Finding your way · Beacons and markers
Books: Engineering & Drafting, Electronics, Power Systems
- **Marker beacons (beginner):** a simple light-and-signal buoy anyone can see and find. Mark a field you like.
- **Field beacons:** report how much of a field is left as it is mined.
- **Coded beacons (advanced):** carry a code, so only the player who deployed one can track it down.
- **Relay beacons (advanced):** pass scanner returns along, so you can hear a field from farther away.

## 2. Seeing what's out there · Scanners
Books: Electronics, Prospecting & Geology
- **Sharper scanners:** a narrower cone that points more precisely.
- **Long-range scanners:** hear large fields from farther out.
- **Assay scanners (advanced):** tell what a field is mostly made of before you fly to it.

## 3. Taking what you find · Mining tools
Books: Mining, Fabrication, Power Systems
- **Grapples and nets (beginner):** gather small pieces faster.
- **Splitters and wedges:** break a large chunk into pieces your hold can take.
- **Cutting lasers (advanced):** slice the really large chunks in the massive fields (rule 30).
- **Dust collectors (advanced):** catch the fine dust a laser leaves behind.

## 4. Carrying more · Holds and hauling
Books: Logistics & Hauling, Fabrication
- **Bigger holds (beginner):** more room for your ore, with real dimensions.
- **Shaped holds:** a long hold for long pieces.
- **Tow rigs (advanced):** haul a piece far too big for any hold back to the Starship.

## 5. Turning ore into metal · Furnaces and processing
Books: Metallurgy, Refining & Chemistry, Ceramics & Glassmaking
- **Space furnaces:** the smelter's own furnace for the smelting service (rule 25).
- **Crushers and sorters:** separate ore from slag before it's smelted.
- **Refineries (advanced):** purer metal from difficult ores.

## 6. Your craft · Ship systems
Books: Pilot Basic Training Handbook, Ship Maintenance & Repair, Power Systems, Life Support
- **Thrusters:** faster turning, or quicker on the throttle.
- **Rest pods:** better sleep in the cockpit (cockpit rest is half the bed's rate today).
- **Power cells (advanced):** run more equipment at once.

## 7. Living aboard · The greenhouse and the water plant
Books: Botany & Hydroponics, Water Management, Health & Fitness
- **Hydroponic racks (beginner):** grow more in less space.
- **Food stations:** meals that help your energy recover.
- **Enriched crops (advanced):** harvests rich enough to sell at the market as food that speeds recovery.
- **Water recyclers (advanced).**

## 8. Your own space · Quarters and personal things
Books: Engineering & Drafting, Fabrication
- **Furniture (beginner):** a better desk, a bigger bookshelf, a sturdier lockbox.
- **Uniform badges and gear** for the closet.
