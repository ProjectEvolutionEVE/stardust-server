# Stardust - Terra Deleta

**The player-run economy of EVE Online meets the creativity of Minecraft: mine real ores, smelt them, design your own blueprints, and trade on a live market.**

The inner planets are gone. Four rings of debris now circle the Sun where they used to be. You start as a trainee aboard the WSC Alexander von Humboldt, a research starship parked by a wormhole beyond Jupiter.

Study in the Starship Library. Fly out and scan the rings by sound. Collect real ores and bring them home. Trade them on a live market. Save and borrow at the Starship Bank. Design your own blueprints, then smelt ore and build at the Maintenance Deck.

Made for the **Zoud GameLab Financial Literacy Jam** (theme: Make FinLit Fun).

## What you do

- **Sell on a live market.** Ore trades on an order book open day and night. Prices rise and fall. Sell now, or wait for a better price and learn what patience is worth.
- **Save at the Starship Bank.** Keep credits in a savings account, or lock them in a 2, 4 or 8-week term deposit for a higher APR. Close a term early and you give up the interest.
- **Borrow with care.** The Bank weighs every loan: your record, your income, your debts and what the loan is for. You see the full cost before you sign. Pay early and you save interest.
- **Choose: sell or rent.** When your share of the cargo hold is full, sell some ore to make room, or rent more space for 10,000 credits a week. Renting only pays if the extra cargo earns more.
- **Design and sell blueprints.** Draw up your own designs. Printing copies has a setup fee and a cost per copy. Sell the copies at auction, and price them above what they cost you.
- **Keep your receipts.** Every transfer of goods gives you a receipt with a code anyone can check.
- **Build a career.** Library books and short tests earn career bars: gold for a perfect score, silver otherwise. Bars earn uniforms, and the uniform you wear gives its skill bonus: faster mining, longer-lasting fuel, cheaper printing, and more.
- **Fly with Inara.** Inara is the officer in the Tower who talks you through every launch, find and warning. She speaks 13 languages, including Arabic and Hindi.

## Download and install

Download the game for **Mac** or **Windows** below. A browser version may also be offered as a "play in browser" extra.

Stardust - Terra Deleta comes from a small independent studio. The apps are not yet signed with paid Apple or Microsoft certificates, so your computer will ask you to confirm that you want to open them. This is expected, and it only happens the first time.

**Mac**

1. Unzip the download.
2. Open the app.
3. macOS says it can't verify the app. Close that message.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
5. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
6. Click **Open**.

**Windows**

1. Unzip the download.
2. Run the .exe file.
3. At "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**.
4. Click **Run anyway**.

## How to play

- **Sign in** with your email address or phone number. The game sends you a one-time code. There are no passwords.
- **Your progress is saved** on the game's server, so you pick up where you left off.
- **You wake up in your Private Quarters.** From there you are sent to the Starship Library first.
- **Pass the library tests to unlock each skill.** The Pilot test unlocks flying. The Mining test unlocks mining. The Trading test unlocks selling on the market.

## Languages

- The app and Inara's voice are in **13 languages**, including Arabic and Hindi.
- The library books and tests are in **English** for the jam. More languages are coming.
