## 1. Where the project stands

Already built and running: the Godot app (flight, true-scale solar system, debris fields, scanner, collecting and
unloading, the live market and blueprint auctions, the Starship Library, the Starship Bank with savings, term deposits,
loans and savings goals, the Desk ledger, cargo-space rental, the Maintenance Deck for smelting and building, the
drafting room with blueprint review and COMMS) and the game server on Railway. The app and Inara's voice are in 13
languages, including Arabic and Hindi; 191 library books are already in Arabic. What it has cost so far is in section 2.

## 5. How sponsor funding would be used

In order of what moves the game furthest, fastest:
1. **People who multiply what's already built:** a senior Godot developer and a 3D artist, so the working systems get
   the look and polish of a top store game.
2. **Arabic first, done properly:** the remaining library books translated and reviewed by native speakers, for the
   UAE and Arab-region audience the jam serves.
3. **Playtesting with real players** of every age and background, so the money lessons land and the economy is fair.
4. **Launch:** store releases on phones, then PC and web, with a marketing push built around Inara and the story.
5. **Running costs** that grow with players: servers, sign-in, voice and AI tools.

**A side note on the engine.** Stardust - Terra Deleta is built in Godot, which is free and suits a mobile-first game.
For the future companion game, Stardust - Terra Nova, we may consider Unreal Engine for a showcase-level 3D world, if
Stardust - Terra Deleta is successful enough to offset the cost.

## 6. Revenue model (for commercial potential)

**Free to start, then a small monthly subscription.** Anyone can download and play free: the Trainee stage and the
first bar of every career. A small monthly subscription (about $4.99 a month, to be set before launch) opens the full
game: all five bars of every career, the drafting room and building at the Maintenance Deck.

- **A steady, known income:** a small subscription from players who stay is worth more than chasing new buyers every
  month, and it grows with the same players whose servers it pays for.
- **Fair by design:** in-game credits are never sold and can never be bought with real money, so no one can pay to
  win. What players earn, they earn by playing, which is the money lesson the game teaches.
- **Room to reach everyone:** the free start lets students, families and schools play without paying.
